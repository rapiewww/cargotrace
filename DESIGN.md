Identity: Nusa Freight, multimodal (sea, air, road) shipment tracking for a freight forwarder moving Indonesian spices and essential oils to buyers worldwide (university final project, Laravel).
Personality: calm, precise, operational. Cinematic maritime photography is atmosphere behind the interface, never decoration on top of data.
Palette dark: background #050B14, surface #0B1728, elevated #11233B, border white at 10%, text #E6EEF8, muted #8FA3BC.
Palette light: background #F4F7FB, surface #FFFFFF, border #D5DEEA, text #0B1728, muted #52647A.
Accent: primary blue #2563EB (hover #1D4ED8). Cyan (#22D3EE dark, #0891B2 light) only for selected or live data. Status: success #10B981, warning #F59E0B, danger #F43F5E, info #38BDF8.
Typography: Inter for the interface. JetBrains Mono only for tracking numbers, UN/LOCODE, container numbers and coordinates. Sentence case; no all-caps micro labels except table headers.
Mood: night container terminal, port lights, global trade network lines.
Imagery: photography appears only in the landing hero and the login panel (dark: public/images/backgrounds/bg-dark.webp; light: a daytime photo, public/images/backgrounds/hero-light.webp, when it exists). Application pages use plain theme surfaces with a faint SVG network motif.
Content rules: no invented statistics; no claims of AIS, real-time tracking or AI; every number comes from the database or is labeled demo data; English UI text; no em dashes. Transport modes are always shown with an icon and a text label, never by color alone.
Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1
